Sunnyside Scrambler

APRIL 2026 HERSHEY, PA 2ND PLACE — WESTERN DIVISION

A ride entry built with CU's Theme Park Engineering and Design Club for the 2026 Ride Engineering Competition. The Sunnyside Scrambler is a small-scale candy-themed ride capable of rotation and 1 ft of vertical displacement, reaching 2G of acceleration — engineered to meet ASTM F2291 amusement ride safety standards.

Role
  • Assistant Project Manager
  • Coordinated communication between subteams
  • Prepared design review materials
  • Co-authored an 80+ page ASTM F2291 compliance document
Engineering Scope
  • Modified and designed parts within an 800+ component assembly
  • Scissor lift and structural design
  • Control panel box design & fabrication
  • Ride assembly and troubleshooting
  • Laser-cut and engraved information plates for the ride and control panel

Challenges
  • Coming into this project with no prior engineering experience, and never having worked on a large-scale build, meant staying current with constant changes across every subteam. Stepping into the Assistant Project Manager role added exposure to technical documentation and to keeping design safety and ASTM compliance consistent across the whole team.
  • Adapting between ideation and creation was its own lesson. Early work went into designing a scissor lift from scratch, which was later dropped in favor of buying one off the market — then reversed again later in the project back to an in-house build. That back-and-forth taught me to stay flexible with designs rather than get attached to early concepts, since they're likely to change.
Results

The finished ride and documentation package earned 2nd place in the Western Division at the Ride Engineering Competition, maintaining continuous operation for 4.5 of 6 hours during competition — including downtime for mandatory disassembly and reassembly.

Co-authored an 80+ page ASTM F2291 compliance document and full documentation package, including a Rider Eligibility Analysis, Patron Containment Analysis, Service Plan, and Risk Assessment.
